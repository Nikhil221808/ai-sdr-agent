## Company
**Pipewise**: deal-risk intelligence for B2B sales teams. Pipewise reads CRM
activity, call notes and email threads and flags deals that are going stale
before the forecast call.

## Ideal customer profile
- B2B SaaS or tech-enabled services, 100-2,000 employees
- 15+ quota-carrying reps, usually on Salesforce or HubSpot
- Strongest trigger: rapid sales hiring, a new sales leader, or a CRM migration

## Personas and what they care about
- **VP Sales / CRO:** forecast accuracy, ramping new reps, no surprises at quarter end
- **RevOps:** clean pipeline data, fewer manual deal reviews, adoption of process
- **Sales managers:** knowing which deals to coach this week

## Proof points
- Customers cut "slipped" deals at quarter end by about 30% in their first two quarters.
- Setup takes under a day for Salesforce or HubSpot. No rep behaviour change is needed.
